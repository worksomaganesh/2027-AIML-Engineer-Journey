## Before submitting

- [ ] Official listing is still active.
- [ ] Current year of study / graduation date satisfies the exact requirements.
- [ ] Location, work authorization, work mode, duration and schedule are feasible.
- [ ] Resume skills are truthful and supported by evidence.
- [ ] Project links open and setup instructions work.
- [ ] Application answers are accurate and tailored.
- [ ] No fee is being paid to secure an interview or offer.
- [ ] Next action and follow-up date are recorded.

## Follow-up rule

Follow up politely only when appropriate for the company and platform. Respect stated timelines and do not spam recruiters. A large application count is not success if eligibility is wrong; review match quality and response patterns weekly.
